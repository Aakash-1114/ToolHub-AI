About ToolHub AI

Tools that respect your time — and your files

ToolHub AI started from a simple frustration: too many "free" online tools bury you in sign-ups, watermarks, and uploads to servers you've never heard of. We wanted something simpler — open a page, do the thing, done.

Private by default

Every tool runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to a server — we couldn't see them even if we wanted to.

Fast, no waiting

No queues, no "processing" spinners tied to a backend. Everything happens on your device, instantly.

Free, no catch

No sign-up walls, no hidden limits, no credit card. If that ever changes, it'll be loud and clear — not sneaky.

Why browser-based

The best server is no server

Modern browsers are powerful enough to compress images, generate PDFs, and build documents without ever leaving the tab. So that's what we build on — it means your data stays yours, and the tools keep working even if our servers are having a bad day.
