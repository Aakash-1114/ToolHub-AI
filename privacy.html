Privacy Policy

Your files never leave your browser

That's the short version. Here's the full one, in plain language.

Last updated: August 4, 2026

The short version

Every tool on ToolHub AI runs entirely in your web browser. When you upload an image, PDF, or type into a form, that data is processed on your own device using JavaScript — it is never uploaded to our servers, because we don't have a server that accepts your files in the first place. We simply have no way to see, store, or share files you work with.

What we don't collect

  • We don't require an account, sign-up, or login for any tool.
  • We don't upload, store, or have access to the images, PDFs, or documents you process.
  • We don't sell or share personal data, because we don't collect it in the first place.

What is stored locally on your device

Some tools (like the Resume Builder and Cover Letter Generator) automatically save your form entries using your browser's localStorage, so you don't lose your work on refresh. This data stays on your device — we never receive a copy of it. You can clear it anytime using the "Clear All" button in those tools, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Contact form

Our contact form doesn't submit data to a server either — clicking "Send Message" opens your own email app with a pre-filled message. Anything you send is delivered directly through your email provider, the same as writing us an email yourself.

Third-party services

To load fonts, icons, and the libraries that power our tools, pages on this site fetch resources from:

  • Google Fonts (typefaces)
  • Font Awesome / cdnjs (icons and JavaScript libraries)
  • jsDelivr (JavaScript libraries)

These providers may log standard technical data (like your IP address) as part of serving those files, under their own privacy policies. We don't control or receive this data ourselves.

Analytics & advertising

We may use privacy-conscious analytics to understand which tools are useful, and we may show ads through Google AdSense to keep these tools free. If we do, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can learn more or opt out of personalized advertising at google.com/settings/ads. This policy will be updated with specifics if and when advertising is active.

Children's privacy

ToolHub AI is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone in that age group.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Questions?

Reach out any time via our contact page.
